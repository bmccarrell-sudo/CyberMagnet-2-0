What We Do

The CyberMagnet Approach

Organic reach only takes you so far. Paid social advertising lets you put your offer in front of a precisely defined audience — by location, interest, behaviour, and demographic — at the exact moment they're most receptive. Businesses ready to invest in paid acquisition to accelerate growth. Most effective for local service businesses, product-based brands, and any offer with a clear conversion goal (enquiry, booking, purchase).

Why CyberMagnet

We treat every pound of ad spend as if it were our own. Campaign decisions are driven by data, not hunches — and every campaign we run is monitored, tested, and improved on an ongoing basis to drive down cost per acquisition.
