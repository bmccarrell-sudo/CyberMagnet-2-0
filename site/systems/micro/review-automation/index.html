What Is This?

Stop Operating Without Knowing What Your Competitors Know.

Most businesses compete without a clear picture of what their competitors are actually doing — how they rank, what keywords they're targeting, what their content strategy looks like, and where they're investing in paid advertising. Without this intelligence, you're making growth decisions in the dark.

The Competitor Analysis Report gives you a comprehensive, data-backed map of your competitive landscape. We analyse your top competitors across SEO, content, backlinks, positioning, and paid strategy — and translate that intelligence into a specific, prioritised action plan that shows you exactly where to focus to gain ground fastest.

What's Delivered

Every Component Explained.

Each component builds a different layer of competitive intelligence — together they give you a complete strategic picture of your market.

🔍
SEO Keyword Gap Analysis
We identify the keywords your competitors rank for that you don't — sorted by volume, difficulty, and commercial intent — so you know exactly where the fastest organic wins are.
🔗
Backlink Profile Comparison
We compare your backlink profile against your competitors' to identify the types and sources of links they've earned that you haven't — and the most achievable link targets for your site.
📊
Content Strategy Analysis
We map your competitors' content output — topics, formats, publishing frequency, and engagement — to identify the content gaps and opportunities in your market.
💰
Positioning & Pricing Matrix
We document how your competitors position themselves — messaging, USPs, pricing transparency, and value propositions — so you can identify differentiation opportunities.
📢
Paid Advertising Intelligence
We analyse available signals of competitors' paid strategy on Google and Meta — ad copy patterns, landing page approaches, and offer structures. (Growth and Premium tiers.)
🎯
Opportunity Priority Map
Every finding is synthesised into a prioritised action map showing you which competitive gaps are largest, most achievable, and highest-impact — with a 6-month sequence to close them.

Best Used For

When to Use This Micro-System.

Competitor analysis delivers the highest value in these specific strategic situations.

🆕
Entering a New Market
Before investing in SEO or ads for a new service area or vertical, map who you're competing with, how they're positioned, and where the gaps are. Start with intelligence, not guesswork.
📉
Losing Ground to Competitors
You've noticed competitors ranking above you or winning clients you used to close. The analysis identifies exactly what they're doing differently — so you can respond strategically.
📋
Building a Marketing Strategy
A competitor analysis is the most valuable input when building or refreshing your marketing strategy — showing you what works in your market before you spend budget testing it yourself.
