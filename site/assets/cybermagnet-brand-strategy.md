## 10. Tone & Voice Guidelines

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short sentences | Long explanations |
| Outcome-first language | Feature-first language |
| Confident, direct | Hype or exaggeration |
| Specific numbers | Vague promises |
| "We build" / "You get" | "We offer" / "We provide" |
| 6th–8th grade reading level | Industry jargon |
| DFW-specific references | Generic nationwide language |
| Active voice | Passive voice |
