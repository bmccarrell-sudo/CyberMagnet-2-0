What We Do

The CyberMagnet Approach

Your website is your best salesperson: available 24/7, never tired, and capable of reaching anyone in the world. But only if it's built to convert. Most business websites look decent but fail at their core job — turning visitors into enquiries and enquiries into clients. CyberMagnet designs and develops websites that are fast, optimised, mobile-ready, and architected around your customer's journey from first visit to first contact.

Who This Is For

Local service businesses without a website or with an outdated site that isn't generating leads. Also ideal for businesses launching a new service line or rebranding and needing a digital home that matches their ambition.

Why CyberMagnet

We don't just design pretty pages — we build conversion-focused sites grounded in SEO best practice from day one. Every site we deliver is keyword-researched, on-page optimised, and structured for Google — giving you a platform that earns organic traffic as well as impresses visitors.
