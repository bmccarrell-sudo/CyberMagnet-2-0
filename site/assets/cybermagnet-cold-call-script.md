# CyberMagnet Cold Call Script
### By Ben McCarrell · CyberMagnet · (972) 338-5778

---

## THE ONE-LINE REMINDER

> **You're not selling marketing. You're showing them money they're already losing.**

---

## THE OPENER

**"Hey [name], this is Ben with CyberMagnet — I help local businesses in DFW stop losing leads and save 10-15 hours a week using AI. Takes about 30 minutes to show you exactly what that would look like for your business. You free for a quick strategy call this week?"**

- If they say **yes** → go to Booking
- If they ask a question → answer briefly, redirect to booking
- If they object → go to Rebuttals

---

## BOOKING THE CALL

**"Perfect. I've got [day] at [time] or [day] at [time] — which works better for you?"**

> Always give two options. Never ask "when are you free?"

**"I'll send you a confirmation. It's completely free, no obligation. If I can't show you something that makes a real difference in 30 minutes, you've lost nothing."**

---

## STRATEGY CALL OPENER

Start every call the same way — before you mention a single service:

**"Before I show you anything, I want to make sure I'm actually showing you something useful for your situation. Can I ask you two quick questions?"**

**Question 1:**
**"What does your lead flow look like right now — are you getting consistent inbound, or is it mostly referrals and word of mouth?"**

**Question 2:**
**"And what's the single biggest thing that feels like it's slowing your growth right now?"**

> Listen. Don't interrupt. Don't start solving yet.
> Their answers tell you exactly what to present.

## PIVOT BY PAIN POINT

---

### 🔴 "I miss calls / can't always answer the phone"

> *"That's the most common thing I hear from [vertical] owners. Studies show 62% of callers who go to voicemail don't call back — they just call the next person on Google. Every missed call is a lead going straight to your competitor. We set up an AI receptionist that answers every call, qualifies the caller, books the appointment, and texts you a summary — even at 2am. Most clients recover 4-6 leads in the first month they would have lost otherwise. At your average job value, that pays for the system in the first week."*

---

### 🔴 "Nobody can find me on Google / I don't show up in search"

> *"97% of local searches never go past the first page. If you're not in the top three results on Google Maps when someone searches for [service] in [city], you don't exist to that customer. We fix your Google Business Profile, build out your local SEO, and get you ranking where your customers are actually looking. Most clients see movement in 30-60 days. At even one or two new clients a month from organic search, this pays for itself — and unlike ads, it doesn't stop when you stop paying."*

---

### 🔴 "I spend too much time on admin / following up / chasing people"

> *"You're doing the job of two people right now. The follow-up, scheduling, reminders, chasing invoices — all of that can be automated. We map out everything you're doing manually and build workflows that handle it for you. Most clients get back 15-20 hours a week. That's not just time — that's the difference between running your business and working in it. At what you'd pay to hire someone to do that work, the automation pays for itself in the first month."*

---

### 🔴 "My website is embarrassing / I don't have one"

> *"Most people check a website before they call — if yours looks dated or doesn't exist, they move on before they ever pick up the phone. We build sites designed specifically to convert visitors into calls and bookings. And we connect it to your Google listing and booking system so the whole thing works together. A single new client from your site covers the cost — everything after that is profit."*

## THE ROI CLOSE

After presenting — before they can object on price:

**"Before you tell me what you think — let me show you the math real quick."**

Walk them through their own numbers:

> *"What's your average job worth? [they answer] And roughly how many leads do you think you miss or lose in a month? [they answer] If we recover even half of those — that's [X] extra jobs, which is [Y] dollars. The service runs [price]. You're at break-even in [timeframe]. Everything after that is straight ROI. And that's before this compounds over 3, 6, 12 months."*

> They did the math with their own numbers. You just facilitated it.

## REBUTTALS

---

### "I'm not interested."

> *"Totally fair — can I ask what you're currently doing to bring in new clients? If what you're doing is working, you're right, you don't need me. But if there's a gap, it's worth 30 minutes to see what's possible."*

Still not interested:
> *"No problem. Can I send you a quick email with some info in case things change? I won't spam you."*
> → Get the email. Follow up in 30 days.

---

### "I don't have the budget for that."

> *"That's exactly why I want to show you this. Everything I'd show you is designed to pay for itself before the second invoice. We're not talking about an expense — we're talking about a system that generates more than it costs. If it doesn't do that, you shouldn't buy it. That's why the call is free — so you can decide that for yourself before spending anything."*

---

### "I already have someone handling my marketing."

> *"Good — that means you're already thinking about it. I'm not trying to replace anyone. When's the last time you actually saw the numbers on what that's returning? Most owners I talk to aren't sure. That's all I'd show you — whether what you're doing is working and if anything's being left on the table. If everything's dialed in, I'll tell you that."*

---

### "I tried marketing before and it didn't work."

> *"That's more common than it should be. Most companies sell activity — posts, reports, impressions. We sell outcomes. If I can't show you in 30 minutes how this translates to actual revenue for your specific business, I'm not going to ask you to spend a dollar. What specifically didn't work last time?"*

→ Listen — their answer tells you exactly how to position yourself differently.

---

### "Send me some information."

> *"I can do that — and I will. But a PDF doesn't replace a conversation where I can look at your specific situation. Can we lock in 30 minutes and I'll send the info as a follow-up so you have both?"*

If they still say just send it:
→ Send it with a follow-up call scheduled in the email.

---

### "I'm too busy right now."

> *"That's actually the most common reason people call me. When you're too busy to think about growth, that usually means you're stuck in the day-to-day with no system working in the background. The call is 30 minutes — early morning, lunch, end of day, whatever works. What does next week look like?"*

---

### "How much does it cost?" (on the cold call)

> *"It depends on what makes sense for your situation — which is what the strategy call is for. I'm not going to quote you a number before I know what you actually need. What I can tell you is we have options starting at $950 a month and it's structured so it pays for itself. The call will tell us which direction makes sense for you."*

---

## CLOSING THE STRATEGY CALL

End with a decision — not a "think about it":

**"Based on everything you told me, [service/package] is what I'd recommend. Here's what happens if we move forward: [brief next steps]. Here's what happens if you don't: [the status quo cost]. I'm not going to pressure you — but I do want to ask you directly: does this make sense for where you want to take your business?"**

- **If yes** → get a start date, send the agreement that day
- **If they need to think** → set a specific follow-up before you hang up

> *"Let's talk Thursday at 10 — does that work?"*
> Never leave it as "I'll follow up sometime."
